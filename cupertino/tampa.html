Local knowledge

Good to know for Tampa homes

High-rise condos

Buildings in Channelside, on Harbour Island, and along Bayshore Boulevard often require a reserved service elevator and a certificate of insurance. Tell us your building’s rules when you call, so we can plan around them.

Older homes

Homes in Hyde Park and Palma Ceia can have tight doorways and older wiring or gas lines. We verify access, electrical service, and gas supply before installation day.

New builds and remodels

Share your cabinet drawings and appliance model numbers early. We confirm the openings and utilities before your appliances arrive.

Neighborhoods we serve in Tampa: South Tampa, Hyde Park, Palma Ceia, Beach Park, Davis Islands, Harbour Island, Bayshore, Channelside, Downtown Tampa, Westshore.

  1. Pre-installation review
